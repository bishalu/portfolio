Two matchers with different failure modes, which is why there are two. Classical spectral
landmarks are fast and near-perfect on clean audio and fall apart under pitch and tempo
shifts; a learned embedding model handles the mangled cases the landmarks miss. Routing
between them is most of the engineering.

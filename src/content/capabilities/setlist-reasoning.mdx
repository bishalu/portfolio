Retrieval gives you the right tracks; it doesn't give you an order. Sequencing weighs key
compatibility, tempo continuity and where a set should peak, then commits to an arrangement
it can justify. The hard part isn't producing an order — it's producing one a working DJ
wouldn't immediately rearrange.

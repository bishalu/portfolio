Two analyses, then one decision. The video side finds shot boundaries and how hard each cut
lands; the audio side finds the beat grid, downbeats and section changes. Alignment then
scores every candidate offset for how many cuts fall on strong beats, and picks the offset a
human editor would have found by ear.
